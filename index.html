Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: 2026-09-22

Yashil Vodiy (uz.yashilvodiy.vodiy3d), published by MAGISTRAL TRANS QURILISH LLC, does not collect personal data. Version 1.0.0 sends nothing off the device: your progress is stored locally only. There are no ads and no third-party trackers, and the game runs fully offline.
