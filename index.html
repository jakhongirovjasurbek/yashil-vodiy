Yashil Vodiy — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-07 · App: uz.yashilvodiy.vodiy3d · Publisher: MAGISTRAL TRANS QURILISH LLC

The game never touches your name, phone number, address, e-mail, precise location, contacts, photos or files. Your game progress is stored only on your phone. There are no ads.

To improve the game, it sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Analytics (Google LLC), which processes them on our behalf:

  • a random identifier created when the game is installed (not a device identifier), used to count how many players come back on later days;
  • in-game events: opening and closing the game, session length, plots and upgrades unlocked, whether the intro was watched, the chosen language;
  • approximate country or city, which Google Analytics derives from the internet address.

This data is not linked to your name or any account, is sent over HTTPS, and is never sold, shared with anyone else or used for advertising.

To delete the data, clear the app's storage on the device, which also resets the random identifier. Analytics data is deleted automatically after 2 months; to have it removed sooner, e-mail j.d.jaxongirov@gmail.com.

The game is not directed at children; the setting is a nuclear disaster, so it is rated for 13+.
